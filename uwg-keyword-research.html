What monthly history changes about the interpretation

Demand is seasonal

Utah “pond cleaning” was estimated at 110 searches in March 2026 and 20 in August. “Pond store near me” was 140 in May and 70 in August. “Swimming ponds” was 110 in July and 90 in August.

One year is useful directional evidence, not proof of a repeatable seasonal pattern. Falling clicks can reflect lower demand; a ranking drop requires its own matched rank comparison.

Averages can hide unusual months

“Water features Utah” averages 50/month, but the history includes 480 in September 2025 and only 10–30 in each later month. Keep it for local intent and existing visibility, not because it reliably delivers 50 new searches every month.

Evidence, definitions and limits

Research sources: 8,400 Utah Google Ads keyword suggestions through DataForSEO; 400 selected phrases checked individually for Utah and US demand; 8,280 distinct available UWG Ads search terms for September 1, 2025–September 25, 2026; Ahrefs US discovery records; the current rank dataset; public service and competitor pages. Candidate screening used business fit, actual Ads activity and relevant Planner suggestions; it is not a claim to have manually evaluated every suggested phrase.

Paid-search evidence: visible search terms total 2,633 clicks, $13,244.26 spend and approximately 47 primary conversion credits. Account-wide primary conversions are approximately 84 “Calls from ads” and 33 “Tracking Lead Forms.” Visible terms cover only about 40% of those conversion credits. The search-term report is partial; it is not a full allocation of account activity. No Google Business Profile or local-action metrics are included in this report.

Conversions: call/form conversion credits are signals, not booked jobs, revenue or distinct customers. Attribution can be fractional; a click can generate more than one action. Small samples do not establish a reliable conversion rate. No conversion for a term does not prove it is worthless, especially for in-store shopping.

Demand: volume is an estimate for the requested region and Google Search network. US volume is context, not Utah opportunity. Similar phrases may share an estimate. Paid-ad competition/CPC is not organic ranking difficulty. Null values remain unavailable. Low-volume specialist terms are retained when the offer fits.

Selection: weekly = existing baseline or a strong business fit supported by local demand/Ads evidence; monthly = additional specialty/product coverage; content = useful educational or cost intent; same-page variant = related wording without an extra check initially; confirm = inventory or offering needs verification. These are editorial recommendations, not a proprietary numerical score.

Measurement next: establish a dated local baseline for approved additions, preserve winning URLs and coverage depth, then log each page edit and compare matched weekly scans. Use Search Console organic queries/clicks and qualified inquiries to validate value when connected. Rank changes and this keyword research alone cannot prove that a website edit helped or hurt.
