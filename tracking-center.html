When will I get an alert?
  • A measured gain or loss of at least 5 positions.
  • A previously ranked page disappears within sufficient returned coverage.
  • The winning page changes.
  • A stable #1 becomes #2 after at least 14 days and 3 observations at #1.
  • At least 3 checks and one-third of a completed daily group move 3+ positions in the same direction.

Alerts suggest a focused confirmation and page comparison. They never automatically reverse website edits or trigger an unbudgeted full scan.
